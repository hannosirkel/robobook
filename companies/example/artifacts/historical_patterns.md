# Historical Patterns

## Example Baseline

- Recurring webshop sales are summarized monthly from WooCommerce.
- Payment processors are used as settlement evidence, not as a second sales posting basis.
- Processor fees are grouped into monthly purchase summaries.
- Printful charges are grouped into fulfillment-cost purchases, with payment drafts created when the matching bank debit is available.

## Practical Implications

- Reconciliation should verify Woo gross sales against processor gross collections, allowing fees to bridge the difference to net payouts.
- Reconciliation should verify processor payouts against imported bank receipts.
- Reconciliation should verify supplier debits against the related purchase evidence before a payment draft is approved.
- Post-submit audit should compare source-derived totals against the final Simplbooks month, not against draft reasoning alone.
